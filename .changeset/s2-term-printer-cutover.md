Split the HTTP endpoint generator into a pure neutral term algebra and printer (spec 0019 seam S2). `generate/term.ts` holds the closed `Term` (`Lit`, `Ref`, `Call`, `Chain`/`MethodCall`, `Member`, `OptionalMember`, `Nullish`, `StrictEqual`, `Cond`, `Paren`, `Obj`, `Arr`) and `printTerm`; `generate/http.ts` builds the endpoint, group, access, problem and payload expressions as terms and prints them at the line-builder boundary. Endpoint fragments and the default `effect`-clause writer are term-valued (`EndpointFragmentPart.call`, `printable` returns terms), so extension authors contribute structure instead of render closures; `FragmentImports` and the `Render` type are removed with the old render path. Import registration and local-name resolution stay at the generator boundary on the existing `Imports` policy, the printer takes a readonly local-name lookup and is pure, terms keep the real `SchemaRef`/`SymbolRef` identities, and a reference's static-member path is structural. Generated bytes, IR and every semantic hash are unchanged.
