# Security policy

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/phibkro/effx/security/advisories/new) or by emailing the maintainer listed in the root [`package.json`](package.json) `author` field. Do not open a public issue for an unpatched vulnerability.

Please include the affected version or commit, the impact, and steps to reproduce when safe to share. We do not promise a response or remediation timeframe; reports are handled as maintainer capacity allows.
