## Observable Admissions outcome and exact accepted contract

A visitor sees currently open periods and application options, submits one bounded application and can follow its confirmation. An authenticated applicant sees only their progress; an authorized period manager creates/revises a department-semester window. For an interviewed applicant `Ja` derives Admitted and `Nei` derives Rejected; a `Kanskje` or returning applicant needs an explicit decision by an authorized Rekruttering decider. The department's delegated school coordinator then announces the outcome **once**, which is final and queues one immutable mail; a delegated coordinator or a global administrator edits, previews and saves the versioned message templates. A returning assistant reads options and registers through their own account, without turning Identity's separate invitation claim into an Admissions endpoint. Keep both **existing** external group names `admissions` and `admissionOutcomes`, their current OpenAPI title/description/tag, and all **18** qualified operation IDs. Do not collapse groups into a new `admissions` superset, add a synthetic nineteenth operation or restore `recordOutcome` (`mw/packages/http-api/src/{admissions,admission-outcomes}.ts`; `mw/packages/sdk/native-api-operations.json`; `mw/packages/http-api/src/api.ts:34-70`; `mw/docs/system.md:164-218`).

`A` below is anonymous External/None/Anonymous without `PersonSecurity`; `P` is `PersonSecurity` with **one** Better Auth cookie **or** delegated Person OAuth bearer, never both. All remain External/Reveal. `CR` is optional `If-Match`/`If-None-Match`; `IK` is required `Idempotency-Key`; `IK+M` additionally requires strong `If-Match`. `D` is dynamic public conditional 200/bodyless 304 with strong ETag, Vary: Origin and the existing bounded equal `max-age`/`s-maxage` (0–30 seconds); `PC` is private/no-store conditional 200/304; `PR` is private/no-store 200; `NR` is no-store 200. Creates return 201/no-store/ETag/origin-relative Location/Vary; entity mutations return 200/no-store/new ETag/Vary; an announcement returns 200 with the private/no-store entity-mutation headers (`PrivateEntityMutationResponseHeaders`). These are exact existing header-schema/response branches, not a proposed simplification (`mw/packages/http-api/src/{access,common,http-semantics}.ts`; `mw/packages/http-api/src/http-semantics.ts:205-212`; `mw/packages/http-api/src/admissions.ts:68-324`; `mw/packages/http-api/src/admission-outcomes.ts:41-356`).

## Authority and application-owned behavior

- **Public reads and submission.** Public catalog/open-period validators depend on current period/department/field facts and a short dynamic cache; evaluate access and preconditions before a 304. The anonymous submit is still a transactional command. Keep the existing body bound, strict decode, origin policy and public rate limit **before** body parsing and receipt lookup, including replay. Within the existing database transaction, lock the command/target/email, check active eligibility, reject duplicate applicant-period submissions, and commit applicant/application/command receipt/audit/outbox atomically. A generated guard cannot become a new identity grant or move rate limiting after a replay (`mw/apps/backend/src/admission/{http-reads,http-commands}.ts`; `mw/packages/database/src/application/postgres.ts:478-648`; `mw/packages/database/migrations/{0002-admission-period-authority,0003-public-applicant-admission}.sql`).
- **Current staff decisions.** Period managers resolve current Organization reach for one department-semester under the read snapshot or write transaction. Create/revise compare current revision and strong If-Match where declared; a successful replay retains the original HTTP capsule and cannot redeliver outbox effects. Applicant progress is confined to the authenticated `profile.owner`. Returning options/registration use `placements.self` with an existing linked person/applicant and history; their command revision lives in the body. Do not merge returning registration into onboarding account claim or infer authority from the UI's navigation role (`mw/apps/backend/src/admission/{http-access,http-commands,http-reads}.ts`; `mw/packages/database/src/{admission-period/postgres,application/returning-postgres}.ts`; `mw/packages/http-api/src/admissions.ts:151-324`).
- **Outcome decision and announcement.** Decide rides the delegable capability `admissions.outcomes` and announce rides `placements.coordinate`, each only through an active team delegation that reaches the department or an active global administrator. A department-board leader, a national-board leader, a team leader and a plain member hold neither, and a board-leader reach is never inherited. Rekruttering may re-decide a `Kanskje` or returning outcome until the announcement; `Ja` and `Nei` derive Admitted and Rejected and cannot be overridden. `Rejected` is refused while the applicant has an active placement in the same department and semester, and a later placement for a Rejected applicant is refused; both orders contend on the same department row lock. Each handler resolves the credential and current authority inside the preparation of the committing transaction, **before** identity and receipt lookup, so a revoked actor cannot replay a formerly accepted decision, announcement or save. Protected reads use a repeatable-read snapshot (`outcomeSnapshotRead`); the read-one handler's **extra Decide check remains** despite the broader declared read AccessSpec. The board is `Decide` (every application, with `canAnnounce` for an administrator who may also announce) or the minimal `Announce` projection with name, state and ETag but no recipient, phone or study data; a plain member is refused. An announcement is final, stores one immutable `{subject, html, text}` envelope rendered from the effective template and queues it once; replay, rollback and denial queue none. Rekruttering's team name, a UI button, an inferred applicant result or a previously observed grant is never authority (`mw/apps/backend/src/admission/outcome-http.ts:99-130,255-300`; `mw/apps/backend/src/admission/outcome-http-support.ts:97-112`; `mw/packages/database/src/admissions/{outcome,outcome-mail-queue}.ts`; `mw/packages/domain/src/{admissions/outcome,authz/delegation}.ts`; `mw/apps/backend/src/http-api/receipt-transaction.ts:196-321`; `docs/research/admission-outcome-policy-evidence.md`).
- **Templates, rendering and mail.** Global defaults are editable only by an active global administrator, and a department override only by that department's delegated coordinator; board membership and the dashboard role confer neither. A global default never masks an invalid selected override. Drafts are a closed ProseMirror document with placeholders, validated by one strict Schema at preview, save and announce, and rendered by a code-owned fixed layout with escaping. Preview and the sent bytes come from the same renderer. The renderer, the validator, the editor component and the mail worker are application code: effx generates none of them, and a generated guard cannot move validation after storage (`mw/apps/backend/src/admission/{outcome-template-http,template-render,outcome-worker}.ts`; `mw/packages/domain/src/admissions/template.ts`; `mw/packages/database/src/admissions/outcome-template-store.ts`).
- **UI and delivery stay put.** The homepage `/assistenter` form and confirmation, dashboard `/opptaksperioder`, `/soknad`, `/tidligere-assistenter`, the outcome board `/opptaksutfall` (renamed from `/vikarer`) with its `/opptaksutfall/maler` template editor, current React Router loaders/actions and their same-origin/SSR generated SDK bridges stay application-owned. Keep existing request identity, error display, privacy/no-store, disabled/retry controls, browser disposal and axe assertions. Admissions has **zero** Foldkit programs, so do not add a fake Foldkit adapter for parity. Period changes, application/returning commands and the outcome announcement retain their current outbox/worker and provider Layers after commit. A loopback mailbox proves transport acceptance of the exact bytes, not inbox delivery, and neither an HTTP success nor a generated SDK call proves delivery (`mw/apps/homepage/src/routes/_home.assistenter.tsx`; `mw/apps/dashboard/app/routes/{dashboard.opptaksperioder._index,dashboard.soknad._index,dashboard.tidligere-assistenter._index,dashboard.opptaksutfall._index,dashboard.opptaksutfall.maler}.tsx`; `mw/packages/domain/src/{admission-period/effects,application/effects}.ts`; `mw/docs/system.md:200-218`).

**Policy is settled by spec 0027 and is not a gate here.** The accepted revision has the two-value outcome, the separate announcement and the editable templates; this spec migrates that behavior unchanged. Do not reintroduce `Substitute` as an outcome, a role or a coverage kind. Dated per-absence school coverage stays Placements-owned and out of this slice, apart from the one added `placement.person-rejected` problem code that changed ten `placements` operations' OpenAPI bytes (`mw/packages/http-api/src/placements.ts:81`; `docs/specs/0027-admission-outcome-policy.md`).

## Proposed generated seam

Follow the current Profile/Directory/SocialEvents/Content/TeamApplications/Organization/Contact pattern: one declaration-only `mw/packages/http-api/src/admissions.effx.ts` and one `admission-outcomes.effx.ts`, each with a distinct `Http.group` on `ExternalNativeApi`, plus app-owned adapters re-exporting the **existing** SchemaRefs, ordered problem tuples, native header schemas (including `PrivateEntityMutationResponseHeaders`), resolver symbols and metadata/access annotators. Defaults contain only facts shared by every operation in that group. In particular, `admissions` cannot have group-wide `PersonSecurity`: four public endpoints use None/Anonymous, while six staff operations use Person, endpoint-local capability/resolver/time; `admissionOutcomes` uses the current Person credential alternatives throughout, but per-operation capability, requirement, resolver, decision time and command headers still differ, and the three problem unions differ by endpoint. Choose `Operation.query` for actual read-only behavior and `Operation.command` for mutations; HTTP POST alone is not evidence of a read or a grant. Of the 18 operations, **11 are Queries and 7 are Commands** (`submitApplication`, `createAdmissionPeriod`, `reviseAdmissionPeriod`, `registerReturningAssistant`, `decideOutcome`, `announceOutcome`, `saveOutcomeTemplate`). `previewOutcomeTemplate` is the single Query-over-POST: it reads, carries a private draft and sample in the body, and writes no receipt, so it is a `Query` with `payloadIsQuery: true` under ADR 0010 and `EFFX2402`, not a Command. Exact paths, group titles, statuses, conditional branches, middleware and annotated schemas remain owned by the native contracts. This is a proposed seam, not committed files (`mw/packages/http-api/src/{organization.effx,organization-effx-adapters,contact.effx,contact-effx-adapters,admissions,admission-outcomes}.ts`; `docs/decisions/{0004-schema-ref,0006-query-is-a-semantic-claim,0010-query-over-post}.md`; `mw/apps/backend/.effx/generated/organization-handlers.ts`).

Add both declarations to the **same** contract and backend compiler projects that currently emit seven groups (`mw/packages/http-api/tsconfig.effx.json`, `mw/apps/backend/tsconfig.effx.json`). Bind the generated typed raw factories at all **18** existing handler functions in `mw/apps/backend/src/admission/{http,http-reads,http-commands,outcome-http,outcome-template-http}.ts`, with deferred guards at each **existing** access decision, preserving public rate limit, snapshot, domain and transactional receipt order (the outcome commands authorize before identity and receipt lookup; a generated guard may not move that). Replace both handwritten `HttpApiGroup` registrations in the root, migrate all backend/homepage/dashboard/type-test imports to the generated canonical exports, and remove obsolete uppercase endpoint names, comments, duplicate tuples and handwritten `HttpApiBuilder.group` branches in one clean cutover. No aliases, dual router, test-only SDK echo, hand-edited `.effx/generated` output or effx runtime DI (`mw/packages/http-api/src/{api,index,admissions,admission-outcomes}.ts`; `mw/apps/backend/src/admission/{http,outcome-http}.ts`; `mw/apps/{dashboard,homepage}/`; `mw/packages/sdk/src/effect-client.ts`).

**Declarations no accepted slice has generated yet.** Observed in the accepted generated contracts, none uses these three; each is a first use and must be proved by its **actual** strict diagnostic and a minimal real reproducer, not assumed: (1) a `PUT` endpoint (`saveOutcomeTemplate`; the emitter documents `HttpApiEndpoint.put` and the transport table maps `Http.Put`, but no mono-web group emits one); (2) a Query-over-POST with `payloadIsQuery: true` (`previewOutcomeTemplate`; covered by compiler tests, never used by a generated mono-web group); and (3) an access with alternative capabilities (`Capability.any("admissions.outcomes.read", "admissions.outcomes.announce")` for the two board reads; the IR carries `One | Any` but no accepted declaration uses `Any`).
